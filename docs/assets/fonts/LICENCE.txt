What they are
-------------
Subsets of the DejaVu Serif family, generated by `tools/make_font.py` (Python standard library
only; no network access) from the copies installed on the build host:

    /usr/share/fonts/truetype/dejavu/DejaVuSerif.ttf        (380,660 bytes -> 27,652)
    /usr/share/fonts/truetype/dejavu/DejaVuSerif-Bold.ttf   (356,668 bytes -> 25,848)

The subset keeps the ASCII range, the Latin-1 letters used by the site's copy, and the
typographic marks in the house style (em/en dash, curly quotes, middle dot, multiplication sign,
ellipsis, arrow). Every other codepoint falls through to the system fallback stack declared after
the shipped family in `docs/assets/styles.css` (`--font-display`).
